I am a researcher in philosophy of mind and cognitive science at the [Philosophisches Seminar](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/fakultaeten/philosophische-fakultaet/fachbereiche/philosophie-rhetorik-medien/department-of-philosophy/department/), University of Tübingen, within the [Philosophy of Neuroscience (PONS) Research Group](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/forschung/forschungsschwerpunkte/cin/arbeitsgruppen-alumni/arbeitsgruppen/wong-h-y-philosophy-of-neuroscience/). 


My [research]({{ "/research" | relative_url }}) brings together perception, social cognition, and metacognition. For this, I combine philosophical methods with empirical work, conducting experiments and collaborating with cognitive neuroscientists.

Currently, I study the sense of reality in perception, and how it may be influenced by others. Besides this project, I have interests across topics in philosophy of perception, mindreading and joint attention, computational approaches in cognitive science, and the science of consciousness.

I obtained my PhD from the [Faculty of Philosophy](https://www.philosophie.lmu.de/en/) and the [Graduate School of Systemic Neurosciences](https://www.gsn.uni-muenchen.de), at Ludwig-Maximilians University Munich, in the [CVBE Cognition, Values and Behaviour](https://www.cvbers.com/) group, and held postdoctoral positions at the [Institut Jean Nicod](https://institutnicod.ens.psl.eu/) and the [Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives et Computationnelles (LNC2)](https://lnc2.dec.ens.fr/en, at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://www.ens.psl.eu/en) in Paris.

In my doctoral project, I focused on the role of perceptual experience in joint attention (see [here](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-020-02602-6)), and on how different sense modalities are implicated during joint attention (see [here](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-020-01766-z) and [here](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13414-021-02347-5)).

I have a BA (joint first honours) in Philosophy and Art at the University of Dundee, an MA in Communication at the Piet Zwart Institute, Rotterdam, and a Research MA (cum laude) in Philosophy at Radboud University Nijmegen, with specialization in Philosophy of Mind and Language. I also completed MSc courses in Cognitive Neuroscience, Artificial Intelligence (at Radboud and Donders) and Neuroscience (at GSN LMU). I have previously been active as an independent [artist]({{ "/art" | relative_url }}), having exhibited my artistic work internationally.

Apart from philosophy and brain sciences, I’m passionate about hiking, rock climbing, swimming, running, abstract art, and have a soft spot for all things avant-garde.
